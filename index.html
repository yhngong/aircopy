About AirCopy

          
        

        

          

            ✈️
            

              100% Air-Gapped & Offline
              
Works completely in Airplane Mode without Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or cellular data. All data transfers optical-only via QR codes.

            

          

          

            🔒
            

              Zero Server Storage
              
Data never touches any server or third party. Everything is encoded and decoded directly inside your smartphone's browser memory.

            

          

          

            ⚡
            

              High-Capacity Animated Burst
              
For texts longer than safe single QR limits, AirCopy automatically loops sequential QR chunks so you can transfer long notes seamlessly.

            

          

          

            📱
            

              Universal Compatibility
              
Single text QR codes can also be scanned by any smartphone's default camera app without installing anything!
